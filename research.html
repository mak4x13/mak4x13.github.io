Smart Security Framework: A Pose-Aware Multi-Stage Enrollment and Recognition System

First Author

Published IEEE Xplore · May 2026

Facial biometric enrollment often accepts poor or partial pose data, weakening downstream recognition. This paper introduces a multi-stage enrollment pipeline that validates head pose in real time against defined constraints before any sample is embedded into the stored representation, improving enrollment quality without adding manual review.

Multimodal Deep Learning Approaches for Monthly Temperature Anomaly Prediction over Pakistan: An Empirical Evaluation

First Author

Accepted (with revisions) ICICAM 2026, MyJCAM Track UniSZA, Malaysia

A branch-based deep learning architecture (LSTM, CNN, MLP) fuses ERA5 atmospheric reanalysis, MODIS satellite imagery, and ENSO/IOD climate indices to forecast regional temperature anomalies. Model performance is benchmarked against statistical baselines (SARIMA, linear regression) using skill-score evaluation across 19 years of gridded climate data.

Beyond Architecture: A Two-Stage Training for Object Detection in Autonomous Driving Under Adverse Conditions

First Author

Under Review IEEE FIT 2026

A two-stage curriculum training pipeline pretrains YOLOv8n on adverse-condition imagery (ACDC), then fine-tunes on a large driving dataset, improving detection under rain, night, and dawn or dusk conditions beyond a clear-weather baseline. The work also introduces a ground-truth-free Temporal Consistency Score for evaluating frame-to-frame prediction stability in video.
