AquaNexus

FastAPI · Next.js

An AI-assisted water allocation simulation for climate-resilient agriculture in Pakistan. Models multi-day allocation for farm agents across Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Balochistan under multiple policy modes (fair, equal, proportional, quota), while tracking crop yield, fairness (Gini index), and reservoir sustainability. Ingests live dam inflow/outflow signals and weather data, with LLM-generated negotiation drafts and policy briefs.

CivitasX

FastAPI · React · Three.js

A city-aware policy simulation platform built to show the impact of a decision before it's made. Simulates how Islamabad, Lahore, and Karachi respond to public policy scenarios like road closures, fuel price shifts, and internet restrictions, tracing ripple effects across transport, economy, education, and public sentiment through an interconnected multi-agent model with conflict detection and AI-generated executive summaries.
